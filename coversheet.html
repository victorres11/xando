Victor Torres - A little background

My original passion was working in competitive sports. After an unsuccessful walk-on tryout at Arizona State, I worked in the recruiting department at ASU - cutting tapes of high school prospects to be passed on to the coaches. Unfortunately, within a few months, there was a coaching staff overhaul and I was let go. As they say - first in, first out. Somewhat regretfully, as an inexperienced 18-year-old, I let this experience jade me. I thought that since I didn't have a substantial network in the sport, I had a low chance of being able to find a real opportunity to make it my career.

Over the past decade, I've built a successful career in tech, progressing from an entry-level customer service role to a software engineer at one of the fastest-growing companies in the industry. While this path was unexpected, it equipped me with a diverse skill set and a knack for seizing opportunities. Along the way, I became fascinated with software engineering, teaching myself the necessary skills and continuously evolving to tackle new challenges.

Throughout my career, I've consistently sought out new challenges and opportunities for growth. From starting at AdRoll in San Francisco to transitioning to their New York office, I've embraced change and adapted to new environments. When I felt I had plateaued in my role, I made the leap to Datadog, a promising startup that went on to become one of the most successful IPOs in tech history. While these experiences were invaluable, I couldn't shake the feeling that something was missing – a sense of purpose and fulfillment that I knew I could only find by aligning my career with my true passion.

After my time at Datadog, I ventured into entrepreneurship, launching my own software products and taking on product management roles at various startups. In these positions, I leveraged my technical expertise while embracing new challenges and responsibilities. It was during this phase of my career that I discovered my unique strength: the ability to bridge the gap between technical and non-technical teams. By facilitating effective communication and collaboration, I helped organizations identify and solve complex problems, streamline operations, and drive meaningful results.

As I navigated my career in tech, my passion for football never diminished. I kept a close eye on football organizations, always seeking an opportunity to get involved and make a difference. Reading books like "The Leadership Secrets of Nick Saban" only deepened my admiration for football coaches and their ability to lead and inspire their teams to achieve excellence. I recognized that football programs face many of the same challenges as businesses, and I became convinced that my unique skill set could help these organizations scale efficiently and effectively. Through my diverse professional experiences, I've honed my ability to identify patterns, solve complex problems, and drive operational improvements – skills that I believe can add significant value to football programs at any level.

To test my hypothesis that my skills could drive meaningful improvements in football organizations, I took on several projects alongside my primary work in tech:

  • I collaborated with the Cal Poly Football program to identify operational inefficiencies. Upon learning that Graduate Assistants spent ~8 hours per week manually preparing spreadsheets for coordinators, I developed a one-step web app that automated 95% of the process. This solution saved the GAs over 7 hours per week, enabling them to focus on higher-impact activities to improve the team's performance and increase their chances of success on game day.
  • When a childhood friend and former professional player launched VSG Sports, a sports management company, I eagerly offered my expertise to help streamline their operations. By implementing a proactive, CRM-integrated system for prospect discovery, I transformed a largely manual and reactive process into an efficient, structured program that enhanced the company's ability to identify and engage with potential clients.

As I reflect on my career journey, I've come to realize that the most fulfilling path forward is to align my passion for football with my professional expertise. I'm at a point in my life where I'm fully committed to dedicating my skills and experience to making a meaningful impact in the sport I love. With my track record of driving operational improvements and leveraging cutting-edge technologies, I'm confident in my ability to add significant value to a football organization from day one.

I believe the AI revolution offers a game-changing opportunity for football programs. By developing custom tools that harness these technologies, I can help streamline processes, uncover insights, and enable data-driven decisions that directly contribute to on-field success. Imagine AI-powered solutions that analyze opponent tendencies in real-time or optimize practice schedules based on player performance data – giving your team that "1 play better" edge when it matters most. My unique combination of technical expertise and deep understanding of football's challenges positions me to deliver these impactful solutions.

If you made it this far, I can't tell you how truly grateful I am for your time. I would welcome an opportunity for a call to tell you more, or even better, fly for a face to face meeting to discuss how I can contribute. Whether it's about optimizing your data pipeline or why the double-wing offense became obselete, I'm game for the conversation. Thank you.
